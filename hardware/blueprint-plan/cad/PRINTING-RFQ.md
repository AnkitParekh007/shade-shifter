# Online 3D Printing RFQ — Shade Shifter Rev A

Please quote two separate jobs from the supplied CAD/STL files.

## Job A — inexpensive fit check

- Material: PLA or PETG
- Process: FDM
- Layer height: 0.20 mm
- Quantity: one front, one left temple, one right temple
- Colour: any/black
- Purpose: dimensional and face-fit validation only
- Please do not glue parts.

## Job B — functional engineering prototype (order only after Job A approval)

- Structural parts: PA12 nylon, SLS or MJF, dyed black
- Diffusers: translucent natural PA12 or milky translucent SLA resin
- Quantity: one set plus one spare diffuser pair
- Critical minimum channel: 3.2 mm wide × 2.0 mm deep
- Minimum cable tunnel: 2.0 mm × 2.0 mm
- Requested tolerance: ±0.20 mm or provider's documented best tolerance
- Deburr all skin-facing edges; do not polish away cable-channel dimensions.

Please provide DFM feedback on wall thickness, trapped powder, warpage, temple flexibility and hinge strategy before production. These parts are an engineering prototype, not certified eyewear.
