# Shade Shifter — Mumbai procurement and supplier guide

**Research date:** 4 August 2026  
**Scope:** Parts, tools, fabrication, eyewear hardware and specialist services for the rapid Shade Shifter POC  
**Geography:** Mumbai, Navi Mumbai and suppliers that reliably ship to Mumbai

> Stock, prices, MOQs and business hours change. Call or WhatsApp before travelling, request a GST quotation, and take the exact specification—not only the component name. No vendor below has been commercially endorsed or technically qualified by Shade Shifter yet.

## 1. Procurement decision

Use four sourcing lanes:

1. **Lamington Road:** development boards, sensors, chargers, passives, wire, soldering tools and basic instruments.
2. **Kalbadevi/Bhayandar/Taloja:** donor frames, plano lenses, hinges, cases and eyewear-manufacturing discussions.
3. **Mumbai/Navi Mumbai fabrication:** 3D printing, industrial design, flex/rigid PCB, optical machining and testing.
4. **Special-order India/import:** WS2812B-2020/1515 LEDs, traceable micro-LiPo cells, ITO-PET, electrochromic coupons, conductive epoxy and specialist color measurement.

Do not buy production quantities yet. For the first bench proof, buy enough for three builds plus 30–50% spares.

## 4. Mumbai shopping plan

### Trip 1 — Lamington Road/Grant Road, one working day

Start around 10:30 after confirming stores are open.

1. Visha World: obtain the core ESP32/charger/regulator/tool quotation.
2. Bombay Electronics/Robodo: inspect micro-LiPo dimensions and connectors; buy only documented/protected samples.
3. Pearl Electronics: price the SMD LEDs, passives and exact ICs; do not accept an “equivalent” without a datasheet.
4. Aavkar: fill common-part, tool and consumable gaps and compare power-supply/multimeter prices.

Carry a printed BOM with manufacturer part numbers, package sizes, acceptable substitutes, quantities and a blank price column. Photograph labels and invoices; immediately record the actual manufacturer part number in the repository BOM.

### Trip 2 — Eyewear sourcing

- Visit/call Prakaash in Bhayandar with a physical thickness envelope or simple CAD drawing.
- Request five donor-frame styles with the widest temples and removable conventional hinges.
- Ask for plano polycarbonate lenses, spare screws/hinges, temple cores and oversized hard cases.
- Walk Kalbadevi/Sheikh Memon Street only for inexpensive donor samples and accessories; treat directory/social listings as leads and verify GST identity before bulk payment.
- Arrange a separate technical visit to Lenstech in Taloja for lens blank/glazing constraints.

### Trip 3 — Fabrication and engineering

- Send the same STEP/STL and drawing to at least two 3D-print vendors.
- Quote tough SLA and PA12 SLS/MJF separately; ask about accuracy, minimum wall, shrinkage, post-processing and reprint policy.
- Send Gerbers plus stack-up requirements to Fine-Line and Velocitech; quote rigid PCB and flex PCB separately.
- Meet an industrial designer only after the LED strip bench proof works; bring mass, battery, antenna, lens-groove and hinge constraints.

## 6. Supplier RFQ checklist

Send every supplier the same questions:

- Exact manufacturer and part number
- Datasheet and revision
- Unit price at 5, 10, 50 and 100 pieces
- MOQ, stock quantity and lead time
- GST, freight and payment terms
- Country of origin and batch/date code
- Return/replacement policy for dead-on-arrival parts
- Compliance/test documents relevant to the item
- Whether the quotation permits prototype cutting/modification

Additional battery questions:

- Cell dimensions including protection PCB and wires
- Nominal/typical/minimum capacity and test conditions
- Recommended charge voltage/current and maximum discharge current
- PCM thresholds and NTC availability
- IEC 62133-2, UN 38.3 test summary and MSDS
- Sample-to-production continuity and cell ageing/storage history

Additional flex-PCB questions:

- Polyimide/copper thickness, finished thickness and minimum bend radius
- Minimum trace/space, via size and coverlay opening
- Dynamic versus static flex rating
- ENIG finish, stiffener material, panelization and electrical test
- Assembly capability for 2020/1515 LEDs and workmanship inspection standard

## 7. Vendor qualification rules

- Buy engineering samples before negotiating volume.
- Do not pay a large advance to a marketplace-only listing without verifying legal identity, GST details, bank-account name and physical capability.
- For critical cells, PCB, optics and skin-contact materials, select on traceability and repeatability—not the lowest price.
- Qualify two sources for every production-critical item.
- Record quote date, supplier, manufacturer part number, MOQ, lead time, landed cost and test outcome in `hardware/bom/supplier-register.csv`.
- Materials suppliers must disclose chemical/handling and encapsulation information under NDA before their coupon touches a wearer.

## 8. Gaps that Mumbai sourcing cannot close alone

The local ecosystem can support the LED experience prototype, frame mechanics, electronics integration and initial testing. It does **not yet provide a verified off-the-shelf solution** for a thin, reflective, arbitrary-color electrochromic layer that wraps an eyeglass frame. That requires one or more of:

- a university/materials-lab co-development agreement;
- a flexible electrochromic-display supplier willing to make narrow custom coupons;
- imported ITO-PET/electrochromic materials with local lamination and sealing development; or
- a Gen-1 pivot to curated segmented colors rather than continuous RGB.

The correct immediate goal is therefore to purchase the LED experience-PoC parts locally while issuing the material RFI internationally and to Indian research groups in parallel.
